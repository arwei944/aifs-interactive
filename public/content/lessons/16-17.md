# Generate agents and emergency simulations

> Park et al. 2023 (UIST '23, arXiv:2304.03442) fills **Smallville**, a sandbox consisting of 25 agents with a three-part architecture: ** Memory flow ** (natural language log), ** reflection ** (proxy generates advanced composition about its own flow) and ** planning ** (daily behavior, followed by sub-planning). The landmark outcome was the emergence of the Valentine's Day party: an agent sowed the seeds of "wanting to hold a Valentine's Day party", without further script, sent out invitations, spread among the crowd, coordinated the date, and the party took place - starting with 24 agents who were unaware of this. Ablation indicates that all three components are necessary for credibility. The recorded failure was a spatial specification error (entering a closed store, sharing a single bathroom). This is the reference framework for agent simulation and multi-agent society assessment in 2026.

## "Problem"

Most multi-agent systems are closely scripted teams: planners plan, coders code, and reviewers review. This applies to well-defined tasks. It fails to capture the sudden, unscripted behavior that occurs when the agent has memory, priorities, and an open world. Research, social simulation, and an increasing number of game AI all require this second type.

The Smallville architecture is its benchmark. Before Park 2023, the best proxy simulations were shallow script followers; After this, this mode becomes the default mode for generating agents in the open world. If you build an agent simulation in 2026, you either use the three components of "Superman Prequel" or explicitly prove why you don't do so.

## Concept

### Three components

*Memory stream. Only additional logs are allowed to record observations, actions, reflections and plans. Each entry has a timestamp, type, description (in natural language), and derived metadata: ** recency **, ** importance ** (self-rated 1-10 by the agent), and ** relevance ** (cosine similarity to the current query).

```
[2026-02-14 09:12:03] observation: Isabella Rodriguez asked me if I like jazz
[2026-02-14 09:14:22] reflection:   I enjoy long conversations about music
[2026-02-14 10:05:00] plan:         Attend Isabella's Valentine's Day party tonight
```

Memory retrieval combines these three scores: the first k entries enter the current prompt.

*"Reflection. Periodically (for every N memories or significant events), the agent generates a higher-order synthesis from the most recent memories. The reflected item returns to the stream and is retrievable like any other memory. This is how agents build "understanding" - architecture is equivalent to long-term beliefs.

*"Plan. Decomposition from top to bottom. First, make a rough daily plan (" Go to work and have dinner with Klaus "). Then there is the hourly plan. Then comes the planning at the action level. The plan is modifiable: when the observations contradict the plan, the agent re-plans the affected segments.

### Why are all three important (Melting)

Park et al. disintegrated observation, reflection and planning. Each ablation will damage credibility:

- Without "observation", the agent will miss the context and act based on outdated beliefs.
- Without reflection, the subject cannot form higher-order beliefs. The interaction is still very superficial.
- No "planned" behavior turns into reactive noise; The target dissipates.

Human raters have the highest credibility scores in all three aspects. Removing any one of them will produce a measurable regression.

### The emergence of Valentine's Day

One of the agents, named Isabella Rodriguez, aims to "throw a Valentine's Day party at Hobbs Cafe at 5 p.m. on February 14th." The other 24 agents did not receive such seeds. During the simulated days:

1. Isabella's plan includes inviting guests.
2. Each invitation becomes an observation in the neighbor's memory stream.
3. That neighbor's reflection led to the thought: "Isabella is having a party."
4. The neighbor's plan includes "attending the party on February 14th".
5. The neighbor told the neighbor. The invitation was disseminated without central coordination.
6. At 5 p.m. on February 14th, several agents gathered at Hobbs Cafe.

This is an emergence in a technical sense: system-level behavior (one party) emerges from local interactions (bilateral invitations + individual plans) without a central coordinator.

### The failure mode of the record

Park et al. clearly pointed out:

- Spatial norm error. The agent walked into the closed store. All the agents want to use the same single bathroom. The agents were having meals in a room that was not used for eating. This model does not merely infer social-physiological norms from the environment.
- Memory overflow. Deep simulation operation leads to an increase in the cost of memory retrieval. Practical remedial measures: Regular memory compression (summarization and pruning) and attenuation of low-importance entries.
- Reflex illusion. Reflection can create relationships that do not exist in the memory stream. Mitigation: Include the source memory id in the reflection prompt and verify it during retrieval.

These are production-related failure modes: Any 2026 agent simulation will inherit them.

### Three components implement the rules

1. Memory is appended. Never change the memory entries. It is precisely the new entry.
2. The importance score is very cheap. LLMS are required to rate their importance on a scale of 1 to 10 when they are being written. Cache scores.
3. Retrieval is about ranking, not filtering. Top k in total score; Do not use hard filters (this will lose context).
4. Reflection operates periodically. It is triggered when the total importance of unprocessed memories exceeds the threshold (for example, 150).
5. The plan can be modified. When a new observation contradicts a plan, only the affected part is regenerated, not the entire plan.

### Generative agents other than superhumans

The subsequent literature from 2024 to 2026 expanded this architecture:

- Multi-agent social simulation for policy/market research. A group simulation similar to "Superman Prequel" simulates users' reaction behaviors to functions. Faster than A/B testing; The accuracy is controversial.
- NPC AI in the game. RPGS with the proxy of "Superman Prequel" generate sudden storylines instead of scripted quests.
- Generate the agent evaluation benchmark. Compared with task accuracy, the metric has become credibility + long-term behavioral consistency.

The architecture is a reference. Expand the exchange component (vector storage for memory, retrieval enhancement reflection, neural symbolic planning), but maintain the three-part structure.

### Why is this important for multi-agent engineering

"Superman Prequel" proves a concept that when components are correct, the cost of multi-agent emergence is very low. This architecture has now been replicated onto open-source models (smaller LLMS will gracefully lose credibility rather than sharply). This shape is used in any production system that requires urgent social behavior. Any system that requires strict task execution will use the supervisor/role/primitive pattern mentioned earlier in this stage.

## This ship

Production simulation rules

- Memory is a database. Choose a real storage (vector DB, Postgres). The standard library in memory is used for prototypes.
- Record retrieval and tracking. For each action, record the top-k memory that drives it. This is your debugging capability.
- Budget each proxy token. The retrieval + reflection + planning for each agent and each tick is an O(k) LLM call. N agents × T beats × calls per beat will reduce your budget.
- Compress memory regularly. Summarize and delete unimportant items. Retention strategy is a design decision, not a detail.
- Clearly identify behaviors that violate spatial/social norms. The architecture does not learn them.

## Practice

1. It is confirmed that more than three agents will meet at the gathering. If the number of potions is increased to 10, will they still appear?
2. Remove the reflection step. What is the behavior like? The map of park melting discoveries in 2023.
3. Introduce a competitive seed objective (" Klaus wants to give a research report at 5 p.m. "). Is the agent fragmented or is one goal dominant? What made the decision?
4. Add space limit: Hobbs Cafe can accommodate a maximum of 4 agents. Can the simulation handle overflow gracefully, or will it encounter the "single bathroom" failure mode?
5. Read Park et al. (arXiv:2304.03442) Section 6 (Emergency Behavior Experiment). Identify a behavior that cannot be replicated in your model. Which component of the architecture do you need to enhance?

## Key terms

| Term | What people say | What it actually means |
|------|----------------|------------------------|
| Memory stream | "The agent's diary" | Append-only log of observations, actions, reflections, plans. |
| Recency | "How new is the memory" | Exponential-decay score by age. |
| Importance | "How much does the agent care" | Self-rated 1-10 at write time. Cached. |
| Relevance | "How related to the current query" | Cosine similarity (embedding-based). |
| Reflection | "Higher-order belief" | Synthesis generated from recent memories, re-ingested as a new memory. |
| Plan | "Day/hour/action decomposition" | Top-down plan tree. Revisable when observations contradict. |
| Smallville | "Park 2023's sandbox" | 25-agent simulation that produced the Valentine's Day emergence. |
| Believability | "The quality metric" | Human-rater score for whether behavior seems like a plausible agent. |
