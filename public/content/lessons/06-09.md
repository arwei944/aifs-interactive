## This question

Text → Music clips ranging from 30 seconds to 4 minutes, including lyrics, vocals and structure. Three sub-problems:

1. Tool generation. The text is like "Low-fidelity hip-hop drums and warm keys" → audio. MusicGen, Stable Audio, AudioLDM.
2. Song generation (human voice + lyrics). "Country Songs About a Rainy Night in Texas" → Full Track. Suno, audio, YuE, ACE-Step.
3. "Conditions/controllable." Expand existing clips, regenerate Bridges, swap types, stem separations, or paint. The spray painting and dry separation of audio are the functional matches of 2026.

### Token LM on the neural codec token

Meta's **MusicGen** (2023, MIT) and many derivative products: conditions for text/melody embedding, autoregressive prediction EnCodec tokens (32 kHz, 4 codebooks), decodes with EnCodec. Parameters: 300 meters - 3.3 meters. Strong baseline; Struggled for 30 seconds.

**ACE-Step** (open source, released by 4B XL in April 2026) expands this into a full-song lyrical generation. The open community is closest to Suno.

### Diffusion on the melt or potential

*"Stable Audio (2023)" and "Stable Audio Open (2024)" : Potential diffused compressed audio. Skilled in looping, sound design and environmental texture. Not good at structured complete songs.

**AudioLDM/AudioLDM2** : Text-to-audio is extended to music, sound effects, and voice through the potential diffusion of t2i style.

### Mixing (production) - Suno, audio, Lyria

Closed weights. It might be an AR codec LM + diffusion-based vocoder with dedicated sound/drum/melody heads. Suno v5 (2026) is the quality leader of ELO 1293. Audio v4 has added a combination of drawing and dry separation (bass, drums, and vocals can be downloaded separately).

### "Evaluation

- FAD (fr<s:1> audio distance). The embedge-level distance between the generation using the VGGish or PANNs function and the real audio distribution. The lower, the better. MusicGen small: 4.5 FAD on MusicCaps; SOTA ~ 3.0.
- Musical sense (subjective). Human preferences. Suno v5 ELO 1293 leads.
- Text-audio alignment. The CLAP score between the prompt and the output.
- Musical artifacts. Non-rhythmic transition, voice drift, and structural absence after 30 seconds.

## Legal Outlook (2025-2026)

- The settlement between Warner Music and Suno. Five hundred dollars. WMG is now responsible for supervising AI images, music Copyrights and user-generated tracks on Suno. Similar UMG solutions in the field of audio.
- ** EU Artificial Intelligence Act ** + ** California SB 942: Music generated by artificial intelligence must be made public.

Safe-to-ship mode:

1. Only generate instrumental music (MusicGen, stable audio open, MIT/CC0 output).
2. Use the commercial api of each generation license (Suno, audio, ElevenLabs Music).
3. Conduct training on the owned or licensed directory (where most businesses end).
4. Use watermarks and metadata tags instead.

## These traps will still occur by 2026

- * * Copyright-laundering note. "Songs in Taylor Swift's style" - Commercial Suno/ audio filters These are not available in the open mode now. Add your own filter list.
- ** Repeat/drift over 30 seconds ** AR model loop. Cross-gradient multi-generation, or use ACE-Step for structural consistency.
- Rhythm drift. The model deviates from BPM. Use the BPM tag in the prompt and the post-filter of librosa
- "Sound clarity." Suno is excellent. Open models are often ambiguous text. If the lyrics are important, use commercial apis or fine-tune them.
- Mono output. Open model generation for mono or pseudo-stereo. Upgrade the appropriate stereo reconstruction (most importantly, Descartes' stereo diffusion).

## Key terms

| Term | What people say | What it actually means |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| FAD | Audio FID | Fréchet distance between embedding distributions of real vs generated. |
| Chromagram | Melody as pitches | 12-dim per-frame vector; input to melody conditioning. |
| Stems | Instrument tracks | Separated bass / drums / vocals / melody as WAV. |
| Inpainting | Regen a section | Mask a time window; model regenerates just that. |
| CLAP | Text-audio CLIP | Contrastive audio-text embedding; eval text-audio alignment. |
| EnCodec | Music codec | Meta's neural codec used by MusicGen; 32 kHz, 4 codebooks. |
