# Text-to-speech (TTS) - from Tacotron to F5 and Kokoro

> ASR converts speech into text. TTS converts text into speech. The 2026 stack consists of three parts: text → token, token →mel, and mel→ waveform. Each component has a default model suitable for a laptop.

**Type:** Build
**Languages:** Python
**Prerequisites:** Phase 6 · 02 (Spectrograms & Mel), Phase 5 · 09 (Seq2Seq), Phase 7 · 05 (Full Transformer)
**Time:** ~75 minutes

## This question

You have one word: "Please remind me to water the plants at 6 p.m." You need a 3-second audio clip that sounds natural, has the correct rhythm (pauses, stress), pronouncing "plant" with the correct vowels, and runs on a real-time voice assistant CPU for less than 300 milliseconds. You also need to exchange sounds and handle code switching input (" Remind me at 6 p.m., daijoubu? ") Don't embarrass yourself with your name.

Modern TTS pipelines are like this:

1. "Text front end. Normalize the text (date, number, email), convert it into phoneme or sub-word markers, and predict prosodic features.
2. "Acoustic model. Text →mel spectrum. Tacotron 2 (2017), FastSpeech 2 (2020), VITS (2021), F5-TTS (2024), Kokoro (2024).
3. Vocoder. "Mel→ Waveform. WaveNet (2016), WaveRNN, HiFi-GAN (2020), BigVGAN(2022), and 2024+ neural codec vocoders.

In 2026, the acoustic + vocoder split blurred the end-to-end diffusion and flow matching models. However, the three-part mental model is still applicable for debugging.

## This concept


*Tacotron 2 (2017). ** Seq2seq: Character embedding →BiLSTM encoder → position-sensitive attention → Autoregressive LSTM decoder emits mel frames. Slow (AR), long text is unstable. It is still cited as a benchmark.

*"Quick Speech 2 (2020). Non-autoregressive. The duration predictor outputs how many mel frames each phoneme acquires. 1-pass is 10 times faster than Tacotron. It loses some naturalness (monotonous arrangement), but there are boats everywhere.

*Witt (2021). Combining variational inference, end-to-end joint training of encoders + stream-based duration + HiFi-GAN vocoders. High quality, single model. Lead the open-source TTS 2022-2024. Variants: YourTTS (Multi-speaker Zero Shot), XTTS v2 (2024, Coqui).

*F5-TTS(2024). Diffusion transformer overcurrent matching. Natural rhythm, zero-shot voice cloning and 5-second reference audio. The top spot on the 2026 Open Source TTS chart. Parameter 335.

*"Heart" (2024) Compact (82M), cpu operation, first-class English TTS for real-time use. Closed words are limited to English, apache-2.0.

*OpenAI TS-1-hd, ElevenLabs v2.5, Google Chirp-3. The most advanced business technology. In 2026, the emotional tags (" [Whisper] ", "[laugh]") and character voices of ElevenLabs v2.5 will dominate the production of audiobooks.

### Vocoder evolution

| Era | Vocoder | Latency | Quality |
|-----|---------|---------|---------|
| 2016 | WaveNet | offline only | SOTA at release |
| 2018 | WaveRNN | ~realtime | good |
| 2020 | HiFi-GAN | 100× realtime | near-human |
| 2022 | BigVGAN | 50× realtime | generalizes across speakers/langs |
| 2024 | SNAC, DAC (neural codecs) | integrated with AR models | discrete tokens, bit-efficient |

By 2026, most "TTS" models will achieve end-to-end from text to waveform. A MEL spectrum is an internal representation.

### "Evaluation

- MOS (Average Opinion Score). 1-5 Scale up, crowdsourcing. It is still the gold standard system; Slowly and painfully.
- CMOS (compared to MOS). A-vs-B preference. The confidence interval for each comment is tighter.
- UTMOS DNSMOS. No-reference neural MOS predictor. Used for ranking lists.
- CER (Character Error Rate) passes ASR. Run TTS output through Whisper and calculate CER based on the input text. Understandability agent.
- SECS (Speaker Embedded Cosine Similarity). "Voice cloning quality."

### Step 2: run Kokoro (2026 CPU default)

```python
from kokoro import KPipeline
tts = KPipeline(lang_code="a")  # "a" = American English
audio, sr = tts("Please remind me to water the plants at 6 pm.", voice="af_bella")
# audio: float32 tensor, sr=24000
```

Offline operation, single file, 82M parameters.

## Use It

The 2026 stack:

| Situation | Pick |
|-----------|------|
| Real-time English voice assistant | Kokoro (CPU) or XTTS v2 (GPU) |
| Voice cloning from 5 s reference | F5-TTS |
| Commercial character voices | ElevenLabs v2.5 |
| Audiobook narration | ElevenLabs v2.5 or XTTS v2 + fine-tune |
| Low-resource language | Train VITS on 5–20 h target-lang data |
| Expressive / emotion tags | ElevenLabs v2.5 or StyleTTS 2 fine-tune |

Open-source leader as of 2026: **F5-TTS for quality, Kokoro for efficiency**. Don't reach for Tacotron unless you are a historian.

## Pitfalls

- **No text normalizer.** "Dr. Smith" reads as "Doctor" or "Drive"? "2026" as "twenty twenty six" or "two zero two six"? Normalize BEFORE phonemizer.
- **OOV proper nouns.** "Ghumare" → "ghyu-mair"? Ship a fallback grapheme-to-phoneme model for unknown tokens.
- **Clipping.** Vocoder output rarely clips, but mel scaling mismatch at inference can overshoot ±1.0. Always `np.clip(wav, -1, 1)`.
- **Sample-rate mismatch.** Kokoro outputs 24 kHz; your downstream pipeline expects 16 kHz → resample or get aliasing.

## Key Terms

| Term | What people say | What it actually means |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Phoneme | Sound unit | Abstract sound class; 39 in English (ARPABet). |
| Duration predictor | How long each phoneme lasts | Non-AR model output; integer frames per phoneme. |
| Vocoder | Mel → waveform | Neural net mapping mel-spec to raw samples. |
| HiFi-GAN | Standard vocoder | GAN-based; dominant 2020–2024. |
| MOS | Subjective quality | 1–5 mean opinion score from human raters. |
| SECS | Voice-clone metric | Cosine similarity between target and output speaker embedding. |
| F5-TTS | 2024 open-source SOTA | Flow-matching diffusion; zero-shot cloning. |
| Kokoro | CPU English leader | 82M-param model, Apache 2.0. |
