## This concept

Z0

### ASR indicator

**WER (Word Error Rate). Lowercase letters, remove punctuation marks, and standardize the numbers before scoring. Use &lt; 5% = equal human speech reading.

**CER (Character Error Rate). The same formula, role level. Used in tonal languages with unclear word segmentation (Mandarin, Cantonese).

**RTFx (Reverse Real-time Factor). Audio is processed in seconds per wall clock. The higher, the better. Long-tailed parrot - tdt hit 3380x. Whisper-large-v3 is about 30 times.

*First-token delay. The wall clock is input from audio to the first transcript token. Streaming media is of vital importance. Deepgram Nova-3: ~150 milliseconds.

### TTS indicator

**MOS (Average Opinion Score). 1 to 5 people's reviews. Gold standard, but at a slow pace. Each sample collects over 20 listeners, and each model collects over 100 samples.

*UTMOS(2022-2026). Learn MOS predictors. It is correlated with human MOS by approximately 0.9 on the standard benchmark. F5-tts: Up to 3.95; Basic fact: 4.08

**SECS (Speaker Encoder Cosine Similarity). Used for voice cloning. Reference and clone output between ECAPA embedded cosine. &gt;  0.75 = Identifiable clones.

*WER-on-ASR-round-trip. Run Whisper on the TTS output and calculate WER based on the input text. Capture understandability regression. 2026 SOTA: 2% CER.

*TTFA (time-to-first-audio). The wall clock is delayed. Kokoro-82M: ~100 milliseconds; F5-TTS: ~1 second.

### Voice-cloning-specific

**SECS + MOS + CER** is a triple structure. Cloning with high SECS but low MOS means that the tone is correct but not natural. The opposite meaning is that the voice is natural but the speaker is wrong.

### Speaker verification

*EER (Equal Error Rate). The false acceptance rate is equal to the threshold of the false rejection rate. The ECAPA of VoxCeleb1-O is 0.87%.

*minDCF (Minimum Detection Cost). The weighted cost of the selected work point (usually FAR=0.01). It is more related to production than EER.

### Diarization

**DER (Digital Error Rate). Missing words, false alarms, and speaker confusion - each one is a score. AMI Conference: DER ~10-20% is relatively realistic. pyannote 3.1 + Precision-2 Business: &lt; 10% of DER is in well-recorded audio.

**JER (Jaccard Error Rate). It can replace DER and is robust to short-segment deviations.

### Audio classification

Multi-label: **mAP (average precision) ** All classes. AudioSet: 0.548 mAP for BEATs-iter3.

Exclusive for multiple categories: ** Precision top-1, top-5 **. Voice command v2: 99.0% top-1 (Audio-MAE).

Imbalance: ** Macro F1** + ** Recall per type **. Report the aggregation accuracy of each class to hide which classes have failed.

### The music generation

*FAD (fm audio distance). The distance between the vggish embedding distribution of the real audio and the generated audio. MusicGen-small on MusicCaps: 4.5 points. MusicLM: 4.0. Lower is better.

*Score by hitting. Use the text-audio alignment score embedded in CLAP. 0.3 = Reasonable alignment.

*Listen to the MOS group. It remains the ultimate conclusion for consumer-grade music. Suno v5 ELO 1293 on the TTS arena (from paired human preferences).

### The benchmark of sound

**MMAU (Massive Multi-Audio Understanding). 10k audio - qa correct.

** MMAU-Pro. 1,800 hardware items, divided into four major categories: voice/sound/music/multi-audio. 4-way random probability 25%. Gemini 2.5 Pro overall ~60%; Multi-audio ~22% in all models.

*"LongAudioBench. Multi-minute editing and semantic query. The audio Flamingo Next defeated the Gemini 2.5 Pro.

*AudioCaps/Clotho. Title benchmark. Spices, cider, defense indicators.

### Stream "from voice to voice"

*Delay P50 / P95 / P99. The wall clock shows the first audible response from the end of the user's voice. Moshi: 200 ms; gpt-40 real-time performance: 300 milliseconds.

*The output is WER/MOS.

*Intrusion response capability. The time from when the user interrupts to when the assistant muts. The target is 150 milliseconds.

def ttr_wer(tts_model, asr_model, texts):
    errors = []
    for txt in texts:
        audio = tts_model.synthesize(txt)
        recog = asr_model.transcribe(audio)
        errors.append(wer(truth=txt, hypothesis=recog))
    return sum(errors) / len(errors)

## Use It

Pair every deploy with a fixed eval harness that runs on every model update. Three cardinal rules:

1. **Normalize before scoring.** Lowercase, punctuation-strip, number-expand. Report the normalization rule.
2. **Report distributions, not averages.** P50/P95/P99 for latency. Per-class recall for classification. Per-category for MMAU.
3. **Run one canonical public benchmark.** Even if your production data differs, reporting on Open ASR / TTS Arena / MMAU lets reviewers compare apples-to-apples.

## Pitfalls

- **UTMOS extrapolation.** Trained on VCTK-style clean speech; scores noisy / cloned / emotional audio poorly.
- **MOS panel bias.** 20 Amazon Mechanical Turk workers ≠ 20 target users. Pay for a domain panel if stakes are high.
- **FAD depends on reference set.** Compare against the same reference distribution across models.
- **Aggregate WER.** A 5% WER overall can hide 30% WER on accented speech. Report by demographic slice.
- **Public benchmark saturation.** Most frontier models are near the ceiling on standard benchmarks. Build an in-house held-out set that reflects your traffic.

## Key Terms

| Term | What people say | What it actually means |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| WER | ASR score | `(S+D+I)/N` at word level after normalization. |
| CER | Character WER | For tone languages or char-level systems. |
| MOS | Human opinion | 1-5 rating; 20+ listeners × 100 samples. |
| UTMOS | ML MOS predictor | Learned model; correlates ~0.9 with human MOS. |
| SECS | Voice-clone similarity | ECAPA cosine between reference and clone. |
| EER | Speaker verif score | Threshold where FAR = FRR. |
| DER | Diarization score | (FA + Miss + Confusion) / total. |
| FAD | Music-gen quality | Fréchet distance on VGGish embeddings. |
| RTFx | Throughput | Audio seconds per wall-clock second. |
