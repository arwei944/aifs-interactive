# Human-responsible scaling strategy v3.0

> RSP v3.0 came into effect on February 24, 2026, replacing the policy of 2023. Two layers of mitigation measures: What Anthropic will do unilaterally and as a framework for industry-wide recommendations (including the RAND SL-4 safety standard). Add the frontier security roadmap and risk reports as permanent documents instead of delivering them all at once. Cancel the suspension commitment for 2023. ## This question

The expansion strategy released by the Frontier Laboratory is partly a technical document, partly a governance document, and partly a signal sent to regulatory authorities. RSP v3.0 is the current Anthropic documentation. It is important to read it carefully, not because compliance with it is binding (it is not), but because this framework determines how laboratories view catastrophic risks and how they communicate and weigh with the public.

The differences between v3.0 and v2.0 are useful units. New additions: Frontier Security Roadmap, Risk Report, and four thresholds for Artificial Intelligence Research and Development. Deleted content: The suspension commitment for 2023. The redefined content is: a two-tier mitigation plan, divided into artificial unilateral and industry advice. The external assessment by SaferAI reduced the score from 2.2 (v2) to 1.9 (v3.0). This is why the scaling strategy has become less strict while looking more refined.

## This concept

### Two-tier mitigation plan

- **Anthropic's unilateral Action ** : Whatever other laboratories do, Anthropic will do the same. Training should have certain thresholds, certain safety measures and certain deployment thresholds.
- ** Industry-wide advice **: What Anthropic believes the industry should do together. Including the RAND SL-4 security standard. These are not commitments from Anthropic. They are policy advocates.

The two-layer structure is not in v2. This means that readers need to check in which column each commitment is located. The safety measures in the "Industry-Wide Recommendations" column are not Anthropic's commitment. This is Anthropic's hope.

### AI R&D-4 threshold

This is the functional level that RSP v3.0 refers to as the next important threshold. Specifically: A model that can automate most artificial intelligence research at a competitive cost. Once Anthropic believes that a model has crossed it, they must release a positive case of determining the risk of bias and mitigation measures before continuing to expand.

According to the v3.0 announcement, Claude Opus 4.6 did not cross it. The document added: "It is becoming increasingly difficult to confidently rule out this possibility." Wording is very important; It acknowledges that this threshold is close enough to draw people's attention rather than being a speculative restriction.

Lesson 6 (Research on Automatic Alignment) and Lesson 7 (Recursive Self-improvement) directly provide this threshold. Automated calibration researchers crossing the research quality bar is evidence that the threshold for AI R&D -4 is approaching.

### Border security roadmap and risk report

V3.0 elevates two types of artifacts to permanent documentation:

- ** Frontier Security Roadmap ** : A forward-looking document that describes planned security efforts, capability expectations, and mitigation studies.
- ** Risk Report ** : A retrospective document of a specific model after its release, describing the observed capabilities and remaining risks.

Both are listed companies. Both are updated according to the announced rhythm. Its utility is that readers can compare what Anthropic says in the roadmap with what they report in the risk report.

### Delete the pause clause

The 2023 RSP includes a clear moratorium commitment: if a model exceeds a specific capability threshold, training will be suspended until mitigation measures are in place. V3.0 replaced the explicit pause with a more gentle expression (releasing positive situations, and continuing if mitigation measures are sufficient). SaferAI and other analysts directly pointed out that this is the strongest regression in the new document.

The policy rationale for this change is that by the 2026 competency benchmark, the 2023 quantitative threshold has been proven unattainable as the benchmark itself has been rescaled. The opposite view is that the pause clause in the scaling strategy is a means of commitment; Canceling it would nullify the credibility of the policy.

### SaferAI has been downgraded

SaferAI is an independent organization that rates RSP-style documents. Their public rating: The human RSP score for 2023 is 2.2 (4.0 is currently the best RSP, and 1.0 is nominal). V3.0 scores 1.9. This has transformed Anthropic from "medium" to "weak", joining the weak category of OpenAI and DeepMind.

According to SaferAI's downgrade factors:
- Qualitative thresholds have replaced quantitative thresholds.
- The suspension commitment has been deleted.
- Artificial intelligence research and development -4 threshold relief is described as a "positive case" rather than specific measures.
- The review mechanism relies on Anthropic's safety advisory group, with limited independent supervision.

### Isn't this a lesson

This is not a lesson about obedience. RSP v3.0 is not a regulation; Nothing can force Anthropic to follow it. Our lesson is to read this document with its due particularity and skepticism. Scale strategy is the main public signal issued by frontier laboratories regarding the catastrophic risk landscape. For anyone whose job relies on cutting-edge capabilities, reading them is a practical skill.

## Key terms

| Term | What people say | What it actually means |
|---|---|---|
| RSP | "Anthropic's scaling policy" | Responsible Scaling Policy; v3.0 effective Feb 24, 2026 |
| AI R&D-4 | "Research-automation threshold" | Capability to automate substantial AI research at competitive cost |
| Affirmative case | "Safety justification" | Published argument that risks are identified and mitigations adequate |
| Frontier Safety Roadmap | "Forward plan" | Standing document on planned safety work and expected capabilities |
| Risk Report | "Retrospective on a model" | Standing document on observed capability and residual risk after release |
| Two-tier mitigation | "Unilateral vs industry" | Anthropic commitments vs industry recommendations, separated |
| Pause commitment | "2023 clause" | Explicit promise to pause training; removed in v3.0 |
| SaferAI rating | "Independent RSP grade" | Third-party rubric; v3.0 scored 1.9 (v2 was 2.2) |
