# OpenAI prepares the framework and DeepMind's cutting-edge security framework

> OpenAI's preparation framework v2 (April 2025) introduced research categories that are different from the tracking category - remote autonomy, sandbags, autonomous replication and adaptation, and sabotage safeguards. The tracking category triggers the capability report and assurance report reviewed by the security advisory group. DeepMind's FSF v3 (with tracking capability level added on September 2025 and April 17, 2026) folds autonomy into the fields of machine learning research and development and networking (machine learning research and development autonomy level 1 = fully automating the AI research and development pipeline at a competitive cost compared to human + AI tools). FSF v3 explicitly addresses the issue of deceptive alignment through automatic monitoring of the abuse of tool inference. Honest tip: Research categories in PF v2 (including remote autonomy) do not automatically trigger mitigation; The policy term is "potential". DeepMind itself stated that if tool reasoning is strengthened, automatic monitoring "will be insufficient in the long term".

## This question

Lesson 19 Read Anthropic's scaling strategy carefully. This lesson will complete this process by reading the data from OpenAI and DeepMind. These three documents are "Cousins" addressing the same issue - when a frontier laboratory should suspend or shut down a model - they tend to be consistent in a few categories but diverge in important specific areas.

Aggregation: All three mark remote autonomy as a capability category worth tracking. All three companies admit that deceptive behavior (false alliances, sandbags) is a special risk. All three companies have internal review institutions. Disagreement: OpenAI divides the categories into "tracking" (forced mitigation) and "research" (no auto-trigger). DeepMind folds autonomy into two domains instead of naming them separately. The laboratory is named Tracking vs Research, or Critical vs Medium, or Level 1 vs Level 2. In different laboratories, the operation results of the buckets where capabilities are located are different.

Reading them together is a very useful exercise. The same ability can be "forced mitigation" in Anthropic, "monitoring without triggering" in OpenAI, and "tracking in specific domains" in DeepMind. Policy stance is very important.

## This concept

### OpenAI is preparing Framework v2 (April 2025)

Structure

- ** Tracking category ** : Trigger capability report (what the model can do) plus safeguard report (what mitigation measures are available). Review by the Security Advisory Group before deployment.
- ** Research Category ** : Model capabilities that the laboratory is tracking but has not yet committed to specific mitigation measures. Including remote autonomy, sandbags, autonomous replication and adaptation, and sabotage safeguards.

The key framework in v2: Research categories do not automatically trigger mitigation measures. The policy term is "potential" mitigation measures. This is a specific delta that requires attention: the position of a category in "Tracking" and "Research" serves as a hub between "gating" and "observation".

### DeepMind Advanced Security Framework v3 (with tracking capability levels added on September 2025 and April 17, 2026)

Structure

- ** Critical Competency Level (CCLs)** : Competency thresholds spanning five domains: Network, Biology, Machine Learning research and Development, CBRN, and autonomy (merged into Machine Learning Research and Development and Network).
- ** Tracking Capability level **: Additional granularity added in April 2026. Specific example: Machine learning R&D autonomy level 1 = Fully automating the AI R&D pipeline at a competitive cost compared to human + AI tools.
- ** Deceptive Alignment Monitoring ** : Explicitly commit to automatically monitoring the abuse of tool reasoning.

The autonomous framework is different from OpenAI. DeepMind did not use "Autonomy" as the top-level domain. It is folded into areas where autonomy would cause harm (machine learning research and development and networks). The argument is that autonomy without a domain is a risk-free ability; The opposite view is that cross-domain autonomy is the meta-risk that the framework should name.

### These three come together

- Internal safety advisory group (named Anthropic SAG, OpenAI SAG, Internal Committee of DeepMind). Conduct a review before deploying high-performance models.
- Explicitly mention deceptive alignment/alignment forgery as a risk category.
- Place the artifacts in accordance with the declared rhythm (Anthropic: Frontier Security Roadmap, Risk report; OpenAI: Capability and Assurance Report; DeepMind: FSF Update Cycle).
- Admit that there is a limit to the defense of monitoring alone. DeepMind made it clear: "In the long run, automated monitoring is not enough."

### Where do they diverge

- **Anthropic** : The suspension commitment was removed in v3.0; The AI research and development -4 threshold is named the next threshold.
- **OpenAI** : Tracking vs. Research Split; Research categories (including remote autonomy) will not be automatically closed.
- **DeepMind** : Independently integrating into other fields; The granularity of the tracking capability level was added in April 2026.

### Sandbag: A specific function that complicates these three functions

Sandbags (a model that performs poorly strategically in evaluations) fall under the research category of OpenAI. Anthropic's RSP v3.0 addresses this issue through evaluation-context gaps (Lesson 1). DeepMind solved this problem in FSF v3 through deceptive alignment monitoring.

If a model performs sandbag processing on evaluations, the capability threshold of each framework will be underestimated. The framework can only work when measuring the work. This is why, in addition to self-assessment in the laboratory, external measurement (Lesson 21, METR) and adversarial assessment are necessary.

### Policy Reading Skills

- Positioning: Every feature you care about should be found in the strategy. If it cannot be found, the insurance policy does not cover it.
- Category: Is it tracking (triggering mitigation) or researching (tracking but not triggering)? OpenAI named it; Anthropic and DeepMind also have their own counterparts.
- Rhythm: Is the strategy updated according to the declared schedule or only after specific events? The rhythm of the announcement is stronger.
- Independence: Is external review mandatory or optional? Anthropic collaborates with Apollo and the American Institute for Artificial Intelligence Security. OpenAI and METR; Mainly internal SAG.
