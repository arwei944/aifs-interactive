# Neural audio codecs - codecs, SNAC, Mimi, DAC and semantic acoustic segmentation

> In 2026, almost all audio generation is tokens. Codecs, SNAC, Mimi and DAC convert continuous waveforms into discrete sequences that can be predicted by transformers. The separation of semantic and acoustic tokens - the first codebook is semantic and the rest is acoustic - is the most significant architectural shift since the audio Transformer.

**Type:** Learn
**Languages:** Python

## This question

Language models handle discrete symbols. The audio is continuous. If you want an llm style speech/music model - MusicGen, Moshi, Sesame CSM, VibeVoice, Orpheus - you first need a neural audio codec: A learning encoder discretizes the audio into a small token vocabulary, and a matching decoder reconstructs the waveform.

Two families emerged:

1. - Codec, DAC. Optimize perceived audio quality. Tokens are "acoustic" - they capture everything, including the speaker's identity, timbre, and background noise.
2. Semantic-first codec - Mimi (Kyutai), speech tokener. Force the first codebook to encode the language/speech content (usually by extracting from WavLM). The subsequent codebook is about acoustic details.

Insight 2024-2026: ** When you attempt to generate from text, pure reconstruction codecs will result in blurry speech. LLMS based on codec tokens must learn the language structure and acoustic structure in the same codebook, which is not scalable. Separating them - semantic codebook 0, acoustic codebook 1-N - is how Moshi and Sesame CSM work.

### Core Technique: Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ)

All modern audio codecs use RVQ instead of a large codebook (which requires millions of codes to achieve high quality) : a small codebook cascade. Output of the first codebook quantization encoder; The second step is to quantify the residuals; Wait. Each codebook contains 1024 codes. Eight codebooks = effective vocabulary 1024^8 = 10^24.

During reasoning, the decoder sums up all the codes selected for each frame for reconstruction.

### These four codecs will be crucial in 2026

**EnCodec (Meta, 2022). "Baseline. Codec waveform overpass, RVQ bottleneck. 24 kHz, 32 codebooks are possible, with a default of 4 codebooks @ 1.5 kbps. It is used by MusicGen.

**DAC (Descript, 2023). RVQ and l2 normalized codebooks, periodic activation functions, and improved loss. The highest reconstruction fidelity of any open codec - sometimes indistinguishable from the original speech of 12 codebooks. 44.1kHz full frequency band.

*SNAC (Hubert Siuzdak, 2024). Multi-scale RVQ - Coarse codebooks run at a lower frame rate than fine ones. Effectively model the audio layer by layer: a rough "sketch" of approximately 12 Hz plus details of 50 Hz. Orpheus-3B is used because the hierarchical structure maps well to LM-based generation.

*Mimi (Jiutai, 2024). The game-changer of 2026. 12.5Hz frame rate (extremely low), 8 codebooks @ 4.4kbps. Codebook 0 is extracted from WavLM and trained to predict the speech content features of WavLM. Password Book 1-7 is the residual sound. This split provided impetus for Moshi (Lesson 15) and Sesame CSM.

### Frame rate is very important for language modeling

Lower frame rate = shorter sequence = faster LM.

| Codec | Frame rate | 1 s = N frames | Good for |
|-------|-----------|----------------|---------|
| EnCodec-24k | 75 Hz | 75 | music, general audio |
| DAC-44.1k | 86 Hz | 86 | high-fidelity music |
| SNAC-24k (coarse) | ~12 Hz | 12 | AR-LM efficient |
| Mimi | 12.5 Hz | 12.5 | streaming speech |

At a frequency of 12.5 Hertz, a 10-second speech only has 125 codec frames - transformers can easily predict them.

### Semantic symbols vs. acoustic symbols

```
frame_t → [semantic_token_t, acoustic_token_0_t, acoustic_token_1_t, ..., acoustic_token_6_t]
```

- Semantic markup (Codebook 0 in Mimi). The content encoded - phonemes, words, content. The loss is extracted from WavLM through auxiliary prediction.
- Acoustic Token (Codebook 1-7). Encoded timbre, speaker identity, rhythm, background noise, fine details.

AR LM first predicts semantic markers (conditional on the text), and then predicts acoustic markers (conditional on semantics + speaker reference). This decomposition is why modern TTS can clone speech zero-shot: semantic models process content; Acoustic models process timbres.

### 2026 Reconstruction quality (bits per second, the lower the bit rate, the better)

| Codec | Bitrate | PESQ | ViSQOL |
|-------|---------|------|--------|
| Opus-20kbps | 20 kbps | 4.0 | 4.3 |
| EnCodec-6kbps | 6 kbps | 3.2 | 3.8 |
| DAC-6kbps | 6 kbps | 3.5 | 4.0 |
| SNAC-3kbps | 3 kbps | 3.3 | 3.8 |
| Mimi-4.4kbps | 4.4 kbps | 3.1 | 3.7 |

Traditional codecs like Opus still outperform in terms of perceived quality. Neural codecs win over discrete labels (not generated by Opus) and generative model quality (what LM can do with these labels).

### Step 4: why AR LM over codec tokens works

For a 10 s speech clip at Mimi's 12.5 Hz × 8 codebooks:

```
N_tokens = 10 * 12.5 * 8 = 1000
```

1000 tokens is a trivial context for a transformer. A 256M-parameter transformer can generate 10 seconds of speech in milliseconds on a modern GPU.

## Use It

Map problem → codec:

| Task | Codec |
|------|-------|
| General music generation | EnCodec-24k |
| Highest-fidelity reconstruction | DAC-44.1k |
| AR LM over speech (TTS) | SNAC or Mimi |
| Streaming full-duplex speech | Mimi (12.5 Hz) |
| Sound-effect library with text | EnCodec + T5 condition |
| Fine-grained audio editing | DAC + inpainting |

Rule of thumb: **if you're building a generative model, start with Mimi or SNAC. If you're building a compression pipeline, use Opus.**

## Pitfalls

- **Too many codebooks.** Adding codebooks increases fidelity linearly but LM sequence length linearly too. Stop at 8-12.
- **Frame-rate mismatch.** Training LM on 12.5 Hz Mimi then fine-tuning on 50 Hz EnCodec fails silently.
- **Assuming all codebooks equal.** In Mimi, codebook 0 carries content; losing it destroys intelligibility. Losing codebook 7 is barely noticeable.
- **Using reconstruction quality as the only metric.** A codec can have great reconstruction but be useless for LM-based generation if the semantic structure is bad.

## Key Terms

| Term | What people say | What it actually means |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| RVQ | Residual quantization | Cascade of small codebooks; each quantizes the previous residual. |
| Frame rate | Codec speed | How many token-frames per second. Lower = faster LM. |
| Semantic codebook | Codebook 0 (Mimi) | Codebook distilled from SSL features; encodes content. |
| Acoustic codebooks | Everything else | Timbre, prosody, noise, fine detail. |
| PESQ / ViSQOL | Perceptual quality | Objective metrics correlating with MOS. |
| EnCodec | Meta codec | The RVQ baseline; used by MusicGen. |
| Mimi | Kyutai codec | 12.5 Hz frame rate; semantic-acoustic split; powers Moshi. |
