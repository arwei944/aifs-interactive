# Voice cloning and voice conversion

> Voice cloning will read your text using someone else's voice. Voice conversion rewrites your voice into someone else's while retaining what you have said. Both are based on the same source language: separating the speaker's identity from the content.

**Type:** Build
**Languages:** Python
**Prerequisites:** Phase 6 · 06 (Speaker Recognition), Phase 6 · 07 (TTS)
**Time:** ~75 minutes

## This question

In 2026, a 5-second audio clip will be sufficient to produce high-quality clones of anyone's voice using consumer-grade Gpus. , F5-TTS, OpenVoice v2, VoiceBox all offer zero-shot or few-shot clones. This technology is both a blessing (accessibility TTS, dubbing, assistive voice) and a weapon (scam calls, political deepfakes, intellectual property theft).

Two closely related tasks:

- ** Voice cloning (tts end) : ** Text + 5-second reference voice → audio within this voice.
- ** Voice conversion (voice side) : ** Source audio (Person A says X) + reference voice of person B → audio of person B saying X.

Both incorporate waveforms (content, speakers, rhythm) and recombine the content from one source with the speakers from the other.

The key constraints for your current shipment in 2026: ** In the EU (AI Act, enforced in August 2026) and California (AB 2905, effective in 2025), watermarks and consent gates are legal requirements **. Your pipeline must emit an inaudible watermark and reject involuntary clones.

## This concept


*Zero-shot cloning. Pass a 5-second clip to a model that has already been trained with thousands of speakers. The speaker encoder maps the said clip to the speaker embedding. The TTS decoder condition for this embedding plus text.

Used versions: F5-TTS (2024), YourTTS (2022), XTTS v2 (2024), OpenVoice v2 (2024).

*Few-shot fine-tuning. Record the target sound for 5 to 30 minutes. lora fine-tunes the basic model for an hour. The quality has jumped from "okay" to "indistinguishable". Coqui and ElevenLabs both support this pattern; The community uses it together with the F5-TTS.

*Voice conversion (VC). Two families:

- "Recognition-synthesis. Run ASR-like models to extract content representations (e.g., soft phoneme posterior, ppg), and then re-synthesize them with the target speaker embeddings. Very sensitive to language and accent. Adopt KNN-VC (2023) and diffi-hiervc (2023).
- Untie the knot. Train an autoencoder to separate content, speakers and rhythm in the latent space at the bottleneck. Swap the speaker embeddings during reasoning. Lower quality, but faster speed. Using AutoVC (2019), VITS-VC variant.

*Cloning based on neural codecs (2024+). VALL-E, VALL-E 2, NaturalSpeech 3, VoiceBox - Train large autoregressive or stream matching models on codec tokens using audio as discrete tokens from SoundStream/EnCodec. The quality of the short prompt is comparable to ElevenLabs.

### Moral issues, not reinforcement

** Watermark. PerTh and SilentCipher (2024) unconsciously embedded 16-32-bit ids in the audio. Survived in re-encoding, streaming and regular editing. Production-ready open source.

*Gates agreed. Each clone output must be paired with a verifiable consent record. "I, Rochit, authorized this voice for X purpose on April 22, 2026." " Stored in tamper-proof logs.

*"Detection. AASIST, RawNet2 and Wav2Vec2-AASIST are used as detectors. The ASVspoof 2025 challenge released an EERs of 0.8-2.3% for the state-of-the-art detectors with ElevenLabs, vall-e2 and Bark outputs.

### Step 2: zero-shot clone with F5-TTS

```python
from f5_tts.api import F5TTS
tts = F5TTS()
wav = tts.infer(
    ref_file="rohit_5s.wav",
    ref_text="The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.",
    gen_text="Please add milk and bread to my list.",
)
```

The reference text must exactly match the audio. Mismatch disrupts alignment.

### Step 3: Use KNN-VC for voice conversion

”“python
Imported torch
From KNNVC import KNNVC # 2023 model, https://github.com/bshall/knn-vc
vc = KNNVC.load ("wavlm-base-plus")
Out_wav = vc.convert (source=" my_voice-.wav ", target_pool=["alice_1.wav", "alice_2.wav"])
```

KNN-VC runs WavLM to extract per-frame embeddings for source and target pool, then replaces each source frame with its nearest neighbor in the pool. Non-parametric, works with a minute of target speech.

## Pitfalls

- **Misaligned reference transcript.** F5-TTS and similar require the reference text to match the reference audio exactly, punctuation included.
- **Reverberant reference.** Echo kills the clone. Record dry, close-mic.
- **Emotional mismatch.** Training reference "cheerful" produces cheerful clones of everything. Match reference emotion to target use.
- **Language leakage.** Cloning an English speaker then asking the model to speak French often carries the accent anyway; use cross-lingual models (XTTS, VALL-E X).
- **No watermark.** Legally unshippable in EU from Aug 2026.

## Key Terms

| Term | What people say | What it actually means |
|------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Zero-shot clone | 5 seconds is enough | Pretrained model + speaker embedding; no training. |
| PPG | Phonetic posteriorgram | Per-frame ASR posteriors used as language-agnostic content rep. |
| KNN-VC | Nearest-neighbor conversion | Replace each source frame with nearest target-pool frame. |
| Neural codec TTS | VALL-E style | AR model over EnCodec/SoundStream tokens. |
| Watermark | Inaudible signature | Bits embedded in audio, survive re-encode. |
| SECS | Cloning fidelity | Cosine between target and clone speaker embeddings. |
| AASIST | Deepfake detector | Anti-spoof model; detects synthesized speech. |
