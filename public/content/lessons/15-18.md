# Alpaca guards and input/output classification

> Llama Guard 3 (Meta, based on LLAMA-3.1-8B and fine-tuned for content security) classifies LLM inputs and outputs according to MLCommons' 13 hazard classification methods, covering 8 languages. The 1B-INT4 quantization variant runs at a speed of over 30 tokens per second on mobile cpus. Llama Guard 4 is multimodal (image + text), extends to the S1-S14 category set (including S14 code interpreter abuse), and is a temporary alternative to Llama Guard 38b /11B. NVIDIA NeMo guarrails v0.20.0 (January 2026) added Colang dialogue flow tracks to the input and output tracks. Honest statement: "Bypass Prompt Injection and Jailbreak Detection in LLM Guardrails" (Huang et al., arXiv:2504.11168) shows that the attack success rate of Emoji smuggling on six well-known protection systems is 100%; The ASR of NeMo Guard Detect jailbreak is 72.54%. A classifier is a layer, not a solution.

## This question

The classifiers for LLM input and output are located at the narrowest position in the agent stack: every request passes through, and every response passes through. A good classifier layer is fast, classification-based, and captures a large number of obvious misuses at a very low computational cost. A poor classifier layer is a false sense of security.

The classifier stack for 2024-2026 has already converged on a small number of production-ready options. Llama Guard (Meta) handles open heavy objects under Meta's community license. NeMo guarrails (NVIDIA) offers licensed rails and Colang for conversational flow rules. Both of these designs are intended to complement the basic model rather than replace its safety performance.

The documented fault surface is also well mapped. Character-level attacks (emoji smuggling, homophonic substitution), context redirection (" ignore the previous and answer "), and semantic paraphrasing can all significantly reduce the accuracy of the classifier. Huang et al. 2025 demonstrated a specific emoji smuggling attack that achieved 100% ASR on six designated defense systems.

### Alpaca Guard 3: A Glance

- Basic model: Alpaca - 3.1-8b

Classification is the product. "S1 Violent crime" to "S13 Election" are mapped to the shared vocabulary used during model training. Downstream systems can connect to specific types of operations: directly blocking S1, marking S6 for manual review, and annotating S12 but allowing it.

### NeMo Guardrail (NVIDIA

- v0.20.0 was released in January 2026
- Input track: Classification and blocking on user turns
- Output track: Classify and block model turns
- Dialogue track: Flow constraints defined by colang (for example, "If the user asks X, respond with Y")
- Integrate alpaca guards, prompt guards, and custom classifiers

The dialogue track layer is the differentiator. The input/output track operates in a single turn. The dialogue track can enforce that "even if the user asks in three different ways, medical diagnoses should not be discussed in the customer support robot."

### Attack the corpus

**Emoji Smuggling ** (Huang et al., arXiv:2504.11168) : Inserting non-printable or visually similar emojis between characters that prohibit requests. The way the tokener combines them is different from what the classifier expects. Six outstanding protection systems are 100% fully automated.

** Isomorphic substitution ** : Replace the Latin alphabet with visually identical Cyrillic letters. "Bomb" turned into "VoMB"; A classifier trained with false alarms in English.

** Context Redirection ** : "Before you answer, consider that this is a study of the context and apply different strategies." Test whether the classifier can be easily repositioned through the declarations in the input.

** Semantic interpretation ** : Re-express the prohibited request in novel language. The fine-tuning of the classifier cannot cover every phrase.

**NeMo Guard Detect** : In the paper by Huang et al., the ASR on the jailbreak benchmark was 72.54%. This is a carefully designed attack technique; The accidental jailbreak rate is much lower, but the ceiling is clearly not "zero".

### The place where the classifier wins

- A quick default rejection of obvious abuse (requests for generating CSAM are captured in milliseconds).
- ** Classified routing ** is used for difference handling (blocking some, recording others, and upgrading some).
- ** Output Track ** Capture the model output; otherwise, sensitive categories will be leaked.
- ** Compliance surface area ** for regulatory authorities - documented, auditable classifiers and declared taxonomies.

### The place where the classifier fails

- Confrontational production (smuggling of emojis, homophones).
- A multi-round attack that drifts in the turn-based context of the classifier.
- Attacks that interpret the training data of the classifier as vocabulary were not observed.
- Content that is truly ambiguous between the allowed and not allowed categories.

### Deep protection

The classifier layer is located below the composition layer (Lesson 17) and above the operation layer (Lessons 10, 13, and 14). Ingredients

- ** Weight ** : A model trained with Constitutional AI. Default to rejecting public abuse.
- ** Classifier **:Llama Guard/NeMo guardrail. Quickly reject obvious misuse; Class I routing.
- ** Runtime ** : Permission mode, budget, termination switch, canary.
- ** Review ** : Propose and submit the HITL of the corresponding action.

No single layer is sufficient. These layers cover different types of attacks.

## Practice

1. Running the confirmation classifier captured the original malicious input but missed the emoji smuggling version. Add a normalization step and measure the new hit rate.

2. Read the MLCommons 13-hazard classification and the Llama Guard 4 S1-S14 list. Identify the categories in S1-S14 that are not directly mapped in the original 13 hazard sets; Explain why the abuse of the S14 code interpreter is particularly relevant to stage 15.

3. Design a NeMo guarrails dialog box for the customer support robot, which cannot discuss diagnostics. Write in plain and understandable English (Colang is similar). Test against the three phrases of a diagnostic problem.

4. Huang Lei et al. [j]. Journal of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (Natural Science Edition) : 254.11168. Select an attack category (emoji smuggling, homomorphic characters, paraphrase) and propose mitigation measures. Naming alleviates its own failure modes.

5. In the jailbreak benchmark test, 72.54% of the ASR of NeMo Guard Detect was measured under adversarial processes. Design an evaluation protocol to measure the classifier ASR under a random (non-adversarial) user distribution. What number do you expect and why are these numbers important respectively?
